A day wtf?

I often get asked, "what the fuck are you doing with your life?"

This website is meant as a general answer to that question.

Mostly: building small things for my own life, then using them every day.

right now

  • earshot: records whatever audio an app is playing, so i can study shows in russian, armenian and mandarin.
  • drumhero: vr drumming. sit behind a real-size kit, sticks in hand, band and crowd reacting to you.
  • plane: an infinite canvas for notes, photos, 3d models and websites. on a quest it becomes a room.
  • thoughts: a place to post a thought without opening a feed.
  • telly: a diary for tv and anime, like letterboxd for episodes.
  • workouts: logging sets at the gym fast enough to keep up with me.
  • focus check: asks every so often whether i'm still doing what i said i'd do.
  • sleep diary: 8:30 every morning, how did i sleep.

before

  • zentweet: open-source extension forcing you to use twitter only to post.
  • post-n-ghost: the same idea, for every site.
  • hustle house: a small hustle-focused house in sf. quiet, pretty, and cheap.
